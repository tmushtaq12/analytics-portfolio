# Dashboard Project

## Overview
This project turns the retail analysis into a concise executive dashboard summary. The purpose is to make business performance easy to understand for non-technical stakeholders.

## Business Goal
Provide a short, clear picture of revenue performance and where the business should focus attention.

## KPIs to Highlight
- total revenue
- monthly sales trend
- revenue by region
- revenue by category
- revenue by customer segment
- sales channel comparison

## Key Findings
- West was the strongest region by revenue
- Electronics was the leading category by revenue
- Corporate customers were the biggest revenue contributor
- Online sales were the main sales channel
- The business is driven by a small number of high-performing areas and segments

## Deliverables
- `dashboard_summary.md` — executive summary and recommendations
- supporting visuals in the project images folder

## Business Recommendation
The dashboard suggests prioritizing investment in the top-performing region, category, and customer segment while maintaining a disciplined view on discounting and margin quality.

## Business Value
This project shows I can translate data into a decision-support view that is clear, concise, and useful to stakeholders.
