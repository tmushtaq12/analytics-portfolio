# Dashboard Project Summary

## Business Goal
Create a clear executive dashboard to track retail performance and highlight the largest opportunities.

## KPIs to Track
- total revenue
- average order value
- revenue by region
- revenue by category
- revenue by customer segment
- monthly sales trend

## Suggested Visuals
- line chart for monthly revenue
- bar chart for region performance
- stacked chart for category contribution
- donut chart for customer segment mix

## Key Recommendations
- Focus sales and inventory around the highest-revenue region
- Promote products with strongest category performance
- Monitor discount strategy to protect margin quality
- Increase targeting for the most valuable customer segment

## Insight Summary
The strongest business opportunities are often in a small number of markets, product groups, and customer segments. This makes dashboarding particularly useful for quickly identifying where to concentrate resources.
