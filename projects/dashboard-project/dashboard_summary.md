# Retail Analytics Executive Summary

## Decision Context
The analysis summarizes UCI Online Retail transaction lines from December 1, 2010 through December 9, 2011. These are historical descriptive results, not a forecast or causal study.

## Headline Measures
- 541,909 source lines were retained in the local audit database.
- After removing 5,268 extra exact duplicates and applying the positive-sale rules, 524,878 lines total GBP 10,642,110.80 in recorded positive sales.
- The sales population contains 19,960 positive-sale invoices and 4,338 customers with an ID.
- The United Kingdom represents GBP 9,001,744.09, or 84.6% of recorded positive sales.
- November 2011 is the highest-sales full month at GBP 1,503,866.78. December 2011 ends on December 9 and is partial.

## Data-Quality Signals
- 135,080 source rows have no customer ID. They remain in overall sales measures but are excluded from customer segments and retention cohorts.
- The source contains 9,288 cancellation-invoice lines. Cancellation and negative-quantity activity is reported separately rather than silently netted into positive sales.
- The source has repeated rows; extra exact copies are excluded from the primary analysis while all source rows and flags remain in SQLite.

## What the Results Support
The sales mix is highly concentrated in the UK. Customer-cohort results can describe observed repeat purchasing for customers with IDs, and RFM scores can create transparent heuristic segments for further investigation.

## What the Results Do Not Support
The source does not provide reliable product costs, profit, or validated margin fields. High recorded sales do not prove high profitability, and the historical patterns do not establish that marketing or inventory changes will cause better outcomes. The final month is incomplete and should not be compared with a complete month without adjustment.
